About

I’m a Canadian civil/structural engineer with experience across consulting, nuclear technology and utility operations. My work has included structural analysis and design, seismic engineering, nuclear facilities and the practical realities of working around long-lived infrastructure.

I completed an MEng in Civil Engineering at the University of Toronto with an emphasis in data analytics and machine learning. That experience changed the kinds of questions I wanted to ask: not only how to design an asset, but how to use data to understand it, compare alternatives and support decisions.

I’m interested in the space where engineering depth meets energy strategy—especially nuclear, power infrastructure, complex capital projects and the analytical tools used to make decisions around them.
